# Founder House — local Company Brain hands-on

Date: 2026-08-18<br>
Duration: 30 minutes<br>
Operator: Manus Desktop / My Computer<br>
Stage language: English

## Promise

> In 30 minutes, you will own a local Company Brain folder and activate its first observed slice:
> one real operation, grounded in evidence, with a first result system, an agent-ready Context Pack
> and one useful output.

Do not promise a complete company map. The sprint may reach V2; V3 requires a later comparable run
and an observed outcome against a measure declared beforehand.

## Architecture to teach

```text
LOCAL FOLDER = COMPANY BRAIN
        ↑
Manus / Codex / Claude = replaceable operator

RAW EVIDENCE → CURRENT MAP → FIRST RESULT SYSTEM
       → CONTEXT PACK → USEFUL OUTPUT → OWNER CORRECTION → NEXT RUN
```

Raw evidence is used for proof, contradiction, exact references and reprocessing. It is not copied
wholesale into the prompt. Each system receives the smallest Context Pack its job needs.

## Participant prep

Send today:

> Tomorrow, bring your laptop and install Manus Desktop. Choose one recurring piece of work that
> still depends on your decision, approval or execution. Bring 2–4 small, recent files about that
> same operation: what the business promises, a trace of how the work happens, customer voice, an
> outcome signal or an approved/rejected example. Remove personal data you do not need.

Facilitator checklist:

1. Open `/companybrain` and test the starter ZIP in an incognito window.
2. Install/open Manus Desktop and confirm **My Computer → Add Folder** is visible.
3. Keep one clean, sanitized demo folder ready with three small sources.
4. Test QR 1 (`/companybrain`) and QR 2 (`/companybrain/receipt`) on a phone.
5. Confirm Wi-Fi, power and one helper circulating.
6. Keep the Web + standalone skill fallback ready; do not debug Desktop for one participant while
   the whole room waits.

## Run of show

### 00:00–03:00 — Frame

> The folder on your computer is the brain. Manus is today's operator. If you switch AI tomorrow,
> the map, context and learning remain yours.

> We are not connecting your entire company. We are observing one recurring operation and making
> it produce one useful artifact.

Show the architecture. Explain state (V0–V3) separately from evidence coverage.

### 03:00–07:00 — Live proof

Use the prepared local folder. Show only four transitions: inspected sources, contradiction in the
map, narrow Context Pack and useful output. Correct one thing aloud and show that the correction is
written into the folder.

### 07:00–11:00 — Install the local brain

> Scan the QR, download the starter ZIP and unzip it somewhere permanent. Rename the folder with
> your company name. In Manus Desktop, open My Computer, click Add Folder and select it.

Everyone should now see `START-HERE.md`, `raw/`, `company/`, `systems/`, `context-packs/`, `outputs/`
and `operations/` in a folder they own.

### 11:00–15:00 — Add the evidence

> Put two to four small files about one recurring operation into `raw/`. Different angles beat a
> giant dump. Do not add a Drive, CRM or inbox just because it exists.

Paste the activation prompt from the page. No separate skill import is needed in the primary path;
the method ships inside the folder.

### 15:00–20:00 — Observe and correct the map

> Find one wrong order, hidden decision, missing exception or contradiction. Tell Manus exactly
> what is wrong and why. File quantity cannot confirm a map; owner correction can move it to V2.

### 20:00–25:00 — Choose the first system and run

> Choose one result system: a repeatable result, trigger, input, pipeline, output, human gate and
> success check. Do not create an agent or automation yet. Prove the manual run first.

The Context Pack must contain evidence, current state, instructions, permissions, output shape,
forbidden assumptions, approval gate and eval.

### 25:00–28:00 — Correct the output

> Would you use this as it is? If not, what is the first change you would make — and why?

The literal reason becomes a candidate judgment rule. Approval is activation evidence, not V3.

### 28:00–30:00 — Persist and commit

Confirm the six files exist in the local folder. Choose the next real case and target date. Submit
only the sanitized receipt through QR 2.

Close with: “Who has a real output, a local brain folder and a next run?”

## QR codes

- **QR 1:** `/companybrain` — download starter and instructions.
- **QR 2:** `/companybrain/receipt` — sanitized receipt only.

Never collect participant files, transcripts, confidential metrics or customer data. Name/email
for follow-up stays in the form/database with explicit opt-in, never in the public brain repo.

## Contingencies

- No Desktop/My Computer: use the Web fallback on the page, import the standalone `.skill`, attach
  files, then download the six seed outputs into the local starter.
- No source: use self-report as V0 and produce a specific evidence-acquisition brief; do not invent
  an observed map.
- Sensitive source: sanitize it or choose another case.
- Slow upload/processing: paste a short titled excerpt or continue asynchronously in the same local
  folder after the event.
- Generic output: inspect which evidence, rule, exemplar or eval is absent from the Context Pack.
- Connector login: stop. Connectors are not the goal of this sprint.

## D0–D7 without another meeting

- D0: first output + next case.
- D1: add only the missing evidence role with highest leverage.
- D3: same System Brief and eval, new real input, no re-explaining the company.
- D7: compare time, corrections, rework, conversion or deadline to the declared baseline.
- Delta observed: V3. No delta: revise context, judgment or system selection — do not add sources by
  reflex.

The in-person event buys speed at three bottlenecks: choosing a narrow slice, separating raw from
usable context and obtaining the first owner correction while the case is hot. The product does not
depend on another meeting.
