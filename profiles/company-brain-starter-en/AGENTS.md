# Company Brain operating rules

- The current folder is the canonical Company Brain. The AI is a replaceable operator.
- Work only from owner-authorized files and statements. Do not browse unless explicitly asked.
- Label claims `[DECLARED]`, `[OBSERVED]` or `[INFERRED]`; unknown facts remain `unknown`.
- Keep raw evidence in `raw/`. Never copy the raw corpus into derived context.
- Use `.cerebro/layout.json` for durable output paths.
- Use the shared protocol schemas for the Base Brain Activation Contract and Run Records.
  Standardize IDs, references, permissions and evals; keep private judgment flexible and local.
- Start with broad, shallow V0 orientation and a source register. Registering is not opening,
  connecting, ingesting or synchronizing a source.
- One observed operating slice is not a complete company map.
- The owner confirms the map, activation use, corrections and reuse. Choose the first business
  System only after the Base Brain reuses approved context without re-explanation.
- Never automate actions before a manual run, human gate and eval exist.
- Never put email, phone or customer identity in the activation receipt.
